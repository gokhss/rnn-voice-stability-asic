## Data-dependent versus synthetic evidence

Synthetic vectors can prove arithmetic, scheduling, storage, handshakes, flush/reset, controls, averaging, interrupts, corner behavior and exact RTL/oracle agreement. They can drive stress activity but are not automatically representative for average power. Genuine held-out voice-quality data is required for score correlation, threshold calibration, false positives/negatives, quality impact of quantization and a credible deployed-workload power estimate. No source in this repository supplies those data or labels.
